How it works

  1. 1
    Draw on the template

    Use the flashcard template PDF in Samsung Notes, GoodNotes, or any PDF annotation app. Top boxes = front, bottom boxes = back.

  2. 2
    Export as PDF

    Save your annotated file as a PDF back to your device.

  3. 3
    Convert & import

    Upload it here, hit Convert, and open the downloaded .apkg in Anki or AnkiDroid.
